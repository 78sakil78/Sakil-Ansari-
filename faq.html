Questions? We've got you.

Everything you need to know about orders, Cash on Delivery, shipping across India, returns and using your SKOVA essentials.

01 Orders & Cash on Delivery

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?

Yes. COD is available across most pin codes in India. You can simply pay in cash at your doorstep when your order is delivered.

How do I place an order?

Add your favourite items to the cart, head to checkout, enter your delivery details and choose COD or secure online payment. You'll get an order confirmation right away.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll help change or cancel it. Once shipped, you can use our easy 7-day return instead.

Is online payment safe?

Absolutely. All online payments are processed through secure, encrypted checkout. Your details are never stored by us.

02 Shipping & Delivery (India)

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is free across India on all orders — no minimum, no hidden charges.

How long will delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 4–7 working days, depending on your location. Metro cities are usually quicker.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order ships, we'll share tracking details by SMS, email or WhatsApp so you can follow it to your door.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver to most pin codes across India. If yours isn't serviceable at checkout, message us and we'll do our best to help.

03 Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We offer easy 7-day returns. If something isn't right, request a return within 7 days of delivery and we'll guide you through the simple steps.

How do refunds work?

Once your return is received and checked, we'll process your refund to your original payment method, or via your preferred method for COD orders.

What if my item arrives damaged?

We're sorry if that happens. Send us a photo on WhatsApp or email within 48 hours of delivery and we'll arrange a quick replacement or refund.

04 Product help

Are SKOVA products medical devices?

No. Our wellness and relief products are designed for supportive, everyday comfort — not as treatment for any medical condition. For ongoing pain or health concerns, please consult a doctor.

How do I care for and clean my product?

Each item comes with simple care guidance. As a rule, wipe electric items with a dry cloth and hand-wash fabric supports gently. Avoid soaking anything with electronics.

Do products come with a warranty?

Many of our electronic essentials include a limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Check the product page or reach out and we'll confirm the details for your item.

Still curious?

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you quickly — usually within a few hours.
